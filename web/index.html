Reading the defense

The call is hidden — read the pre-snap look instead: one-high safety with corners off is Cover 3, one-high with press is Man Free, two-high squat is Cover 2.

Staff sometimes disguise the look and rotate after the snap.

Run it well and play-action opens up — throw while PA WINDOW is showing.
